About Us

Darjeeling Public School — a name that needs no introduction in the field of quality education.

Darjeeling Public School has taken a giant leap towards excellence even in the remotest parts of the country. It is a collective effort of a few visionaries to offer better education facilities where development needs a boost.

Quality education goes beyond theoretical knowledge. At Darjeeling Public School, we place equal emphasis on values and skills that help young minds evolve into better individuals and responsible citizens.

Our commitment is to nurture academic excellence, character, discipline, and confidence so every child is prepared for a bright future.
